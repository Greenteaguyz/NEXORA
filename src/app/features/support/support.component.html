🛡️ Privacy & Data Trust Notice

NEXORA values developer freedom and player privacy. The marketplace is engineered with transparent, zero-telemetry local storage and open distribution standards.

100% DRM-Free
Every game package you acquire is yours forever. No launcher lock-in, no background online checks, and no activation keys required.
Local Browser Persistence
All user sessions, library states, and wishlists are stored locally in your browser via IndexedDB & LocalStorage.
Simulated Transactions
All monetary transactions on this prototype are 100% simulated sandbox mock purchases with zero real credit card charges.
